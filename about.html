Enterprise Profile

Built on Four Decades of Bullion Integrity & Modern Technology

La Esvastica Pvt. Ltd. is an Ahmedabad-based diversified enterprise operating across gold testing services, digital marketing, and watch sales and repair.

The company provides specialized digital solutions including SEO, graphic design, video editing, and business analytics, while maintaining a trusted presence in the precision watch retail and repair sector in Khadia, Ahmedabad.

Our primary growth focus is gold testing services through institutional partnerships, including our existing association with Bank of India. Led by Sanjay Soni and Jeel Soni, the company aims to expand its banking partnerships, strengthen operations, and build a scalable, audit-compliant business across India.
