Non-Destructive XRF Spectrometry: The Modern Gold Standard

Traditional gold loan valuation relies on touchstones and acid testing—subjective techniques that damage customer ornaments and frequently fail to detect deceptive alloy compositions, thick plating, or tungsten cores.

La Esvastica deploys state-of-the-art benchtop XRF (X-Ray Fluorescence) analyzers. By emitting a precise primary X-ray beam onto the jewelry piece, individual atoms emit characteristic secondary fluorescence rays that instantly identify the exact percentage of Gold (Au), Silver (Ag), Copper (Cu), Zinc (Zn), and other base or hazardous metals without scratching or melting a single milligram.

  • Zero Weight Loss: 100% non-destructive, preserving the exact cosmetic and physical integrity of pledged ornaments.
  • Elemental Precision: Simultaneously measures up to 24 elements with accuracy up to ±0.05% (0.01 Karat).
  • Rapid Results: Fully automated digital readout produced in just 15 to 30 seconds for immediate loan decisions.
