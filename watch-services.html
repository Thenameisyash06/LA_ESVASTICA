Comprehensive Care

Our Horological Repair & Maintenance Services

Every watch is inspected under magnification, tested for water resistance, and calibrated for optimal timekeeping.

Mechanical & Automatic Movement Overhaul

Complete disassembly, ultrasonic cleaning of gear trains and escapements, microscopic lubrication using synthetic Swiss oils, and digital timing calibration on a computerized Witschi-grade microphone.

Battery Replacement & Pressure Testing

Installation of high-drain, leak-resistant silver-oxide batteries paired with case-back gasket inspection, silicon lubrication, and vacuum water-resistance testing to safeguard your watch against moisture damage.

Ultrasonic Case & Bracelet Cleaning

Deep ultrasonic acoustic cleaning in specialized degreasing solutions removes embedded dirt, sweat residues, and grime from between bracelet links, restoring the factory shine and luster of steel, gold, and bimetal bands.

Crystal Replacement & Dial Restoration

Custom fitting of scratch-resistant sapphire crystals, mineral glass, and vintage acrylic domed crystals. Careful dial re-alignment, hand straightening, and index luminescent application.

Strap Resizing, Clasp & Stem Repair

Link removal and micro-adjustment for custom wrist comfort, crown and winding stem replacement, spring bar reinforcement, and clasp tension adjustment to prevent accidental drops.
