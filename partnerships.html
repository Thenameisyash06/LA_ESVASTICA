Risk Mitigation

Securing India's ₹10+ Lakh Crore Gold Loan Ecosystem

With household gold holdings exceeding 25,000 tonnes in India and surging gold loan disbursements, institutional lenders face three critical collateral challenges that La Esvastica systematically eliminates.

Under-Caratage & Plating Fraud

Traditional acid rubs only test surface layers. Counterfeiters exploit this using heavy gold plating over brass or copper. Our XRF technology detects underlying substrate adulterations instantly.

Audit Compliance & Digital Receipts

Every assessment automatically produces a timestamped digital analysis record indicating exact Karatage, Gold % (Au), and secondary metals, creating a bulletproof audit trail for internal inspectors.

Disbursement Acceleration

Reduce gold loan processing times from 45 minutes to under 5 minutes per applicant. Instant testing enhances borrower satisfaction and allows branches to handle 4x higher loan volume.
